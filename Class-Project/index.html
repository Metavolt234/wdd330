GET TO KNOW THE PLACE

Small on the map.
Huge on feeling.

Uganda sits in East Africa, straddling the equator and sharing the waters of Lake Victoria. Its landscapes run from the Rwenzori Mountains to savannah, forest and the River Nile.

This guide is a starting point for curious travellers, not a substitute for local advice. Park rules, access, permits and conditions can change; check current details with official park authorities and local operators before you go.
